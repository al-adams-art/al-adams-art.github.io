COMMISSIONS

Commissioned work is available across altered objects, miniature worlds, painting, assemblage, and mixed media.

Process

HOW IT WORKS

  • Describe the project, what matters about it, and any materials or references already available.
  • Include a realistic budget range.
  • Timing, shipping, scope, and price are confirmed before work begins.
  • Reference images are useful. Direct copying of another artist’s work is not offered.
  • Unusual requests are welcome if the project can be defined clearly enough to build.
