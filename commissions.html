COMMISSIONS

Bring an object, an idea, a story, a room in miniature, a half-formed image, or something weird enough that you are not sure which category it belongs in.

Before anybody panics

HOW IT WORKS

  • Describe what you want, what matters about it, and what you already have.
  • Include the budget range you are actually comfortable with.
  • Timing, shipping, scope, and price are confirmed before work begins.
  • Reference images are useful. Copying somebody else’s work is not the assignment.
  • Strange requests are welcome. Vague requests are survivable.
