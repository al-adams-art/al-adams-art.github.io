ABOUT A. L.

Collector, artist, writer, master of the side arts.

“What has it seen? Who held it? What secrets does it know?”

A. L. Adams makes art from fragments, stories, symbols, strange observations, and the things most people leave behind. The work moves between paintings, altered objects, miniature worlds, assemblage, found materials, and visual storytelling.

The common thread is not a tidy aesthetic category. It is attention. Old things have previous lives. Damage can become information. Tiny details matter even when nobody is guaranteed to notice them. The interesting part is often the thing hidden on the inside of the drawer.

There is no invented exhibition history here, no inflated credentials, and no heroic artist mythology. The work gets to make the case.

“I find things with personality, things that still have life in them, things that don't make my brain go, ugh.”
“If I can cut it out and I can glue it down, it's going on the thing if it's interesting enough or useful.”
“And we deserve something cool and nice and that makes us smile.”
