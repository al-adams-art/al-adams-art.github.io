02 / The hand behind the wreckage

THE HAND BEHIND THE WRECKAGE

A. L. Adams makes art from fragments, stories, symbols, strange observations, and the things most people leave behind. The work moves between dark whimsy, visual storytelling, emotional archaeology, and beautiful disorder.

This page does not invent biography, exhibitions, credentials, or heroic mythology. The work gets to make the case.
“Collector, artist, writer, master of the side arts.”
